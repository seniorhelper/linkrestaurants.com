Questions & answers

Menu Pro questions

What is the difference between Menu Pro and the $99 setup?
The $99 setup builds your menu page and one NFC card, then you pay $25 a month for hosting and $100 for each full menu update. Menu Pro rolls hosting, a full menu update every quarter, NFC table tags and Featured placement on our restaurant guides into one plan at $49 a month or $490 a year.
How fast does my Menu Pro page go live?
Within 48 hours of receiving your menu. Send photos of your printed menu, a PDF or a pasted document. We build the page, send you a preview to approve, and publish it. NFC table tags ship after you approve the page.
Does Menu Pro guarantee more customers or a Google ranking?
No, and nobody honest can. What we promise is the work: a fast menu page built as real text with Menu structured data, kept current every quarter, linked from your table tags and from our guide sites with a Featured badge. That gives search engines and diners something accurate to find.
Can I cancel Menu Pro?
Yes. The monthly plan can be cancelled anytime online and stops at the end of the month you have paid for. The yearly plan covers twelve months for one payment of $490, which works out to two months free compared with paying monthly.
What counts as a quarterly menu update?
Once each quarter you can send us a full set of changes: new dishes, new prices, new sections or a seasonal menu. We rewrite the page, re-check the structured data and publish it. Quick fixes in between, like a sold-out special or a holiday hours note, are welcome too.
Where does the Featured badge appear?
On the restaurant guide sites we publish for the market your restaurant is in, such as Honolulu, Denver, Colorado, Belize, Cabarete and Puerto Plata. Featured listings are placed near the top of the relevant guide page and clearly labelled Featured, as paid placements should be.
