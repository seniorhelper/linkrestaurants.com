On the water in San Pedro

One of the best beach bars in San Pedro, Belize

Wayo's Beach Bar sits right on the sea in San Pedro Town on Ambergris Caye, and it has earned its spot as one of the island's favourite waterfront hangouts. Built out over the water at 99 Boca del Rio Drive, it's the kind of place where the deck is the dining room, the breeze is the air conditioning, and the sunset is part of the meal. With a 4.7-star rating from more than 500 guests, it's become a go-to for both visitors stepping off the water taxi and locals who know where the good times are.

The kitchen leans into what Belize does best: fresh Caribbean seafood. Order the ceviche — a true Belizean must-have, made with conch, shrimp or a combo tossed in fresh lime juice — or the fresh fish filet of the day, grilled with garlic butter, blackened, jerk, or done "fisherman style" in foil with peppers and onions. Landlubbers are covered too, with smashed beef sliders, Wayo's curry, fajitas, tacos, and a stack of small plates built for sharing over a few rounds.

A full bar with cocktails and live music

Wayo's is as much a bar as it is a grill. It's known island-wide for great cocktails, happy-hour food and live music, making it one of the top spots for an evening out in San Pedro. Whether you're winding down after a day of diving the Belize Barrier Reef, coming in from a fishing charter, or just chasing the best sunset view on the caye, the drinks are cold and the vibe is easy.

You catch it, they'll cook it

One thing that sets Wayo's apart from other San Pedro restaurants is its "You Catch, We Cook" service. Spend the day out with a local fishing guide, bring back your catch, and the kitchen will prepare it fresh for just $25 per person. It's a favourite for anglers who want to taste the reef the same day they reel it in — the freshest fish dinner you can get on Ambergris Caye.

Prices on the full menu are in Belize dollars and already include the 12.5% GST, so what you see is what you pay. Ready to head over? Call Wayo's at 226-2035 or tap directions above. The bar opens daily at 11am and runs right through happy hour into the night.

Good to know

Wayo's Beach Bar — questions & answers

Where is Wayo's Beach Bar located?
Wayo's is on the water at 99 Boca del Rio Drive in San Pedro Town, on Ambergris Caye, Belize. It's a beachfront bar and grill built out over the sea, just north of the town centre.
What are Wayo's opening hours?
Wayo's opens daily from around 11am and stays open through the evening for happy hour, dinner and live music. Hours can shift with the season, so call ahead at 226-2035 if you're planning a late visit.
How much does it cost to eat at Wayo's?
Most guests spend around BZ$20–40 per person. All prices on the menu are in Belize dollars and already include the 12.5% GST, so there are no surprises on the bill.
What kind of food does Wayo's serve?
Fresh Caribbean and Belizean seafood is the star — ceviche, fresh fish of the day, coconut shrimp and conch chowder — alongside sliders, tacos, fajitas, pasta, salads, curry and shareable small plates. There are vegetarian options too, including a veggie burger and veggie tacos.
Does Wayo's have a full bar?
Yes. Wayo's is a full bar known for great cocktails and happy-hour specials, right on the water. It's one of the most popular spots in San Pedro for drinks at sunset.
What is the "You Catch We Cook" service?
If you've been out fishing with a local guide, bring your catch to Wayo's and the kitchen will cook it fresh for $25 per person (2 person minimum), served with one side per person. It's the freshest fish dinner on the island.
Is there live music at Wayo's?
Yes — live music is one of the things Wayo's is known for, along with its happy-hour food and cocktails. It's a lively spot in the evenings.
What's the phone number for Wayo's Beach Bar?
You can reach Wayo's at 226-2035. Tap the call button at the top of this page to dial directly.
